# Kindergarten and Grade 1 reading plan: topic map

The two supplied IXL reading plans are checklists, not score conversions. Repeated score-band rows and different word lists are grouped here by the task they teach; this table does not assert a one-to-one copy of IXL activities. “Partial” identifies a task for which the app has related practice but not all listed formats, word patterns, or text types. An assessment samples questions and is not guaranteed to cover every row. The app does not provide an official i-Ready score or readiness prediction.

| Plan topic or task family | App skill IDs | Coverage and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rhyming: recognize and choose rhymes; odd one out, complete rhymes and poems | `r.rhyme` | Partial: recognition is available; composing a poem, spelling rhyming answers and all listed formats are not. |
| Syllables: count, compare, sort, combine | `r.syllables`, `r.syllable-compare`, `r1.syllable-join` | Count and compare, plus joining selected spoken word parts. Sorting multiple items and general syllable segmentation are not present. |
| Sound awareness: first/final sounds and same sounds | `r.firstsound`, `r.lastsound`, `r.sound-position` | Identify first and final sounds; formats vary from the plan. |
| Sound awareness: blend, segment and order phonemes | `r.blend`, `r.segment`, `r.sound-position` | Partial: blending, counting and selected positions; arranging every phoneme in order is not supported. |
| Identify short vowels, compare long/short and same vowel sounds | `r.vowelsounds`, `r1.long-short` | Selected listening examples; no drag-and-sort format. |
| Alphabet: identify upper/lowercase, match cases and confusing letters | `r.letters` | Generated recognition and matching; no select-all or alphabet-order task. |
| Vowels versus consonants; find vowel letters | `r.vowel-letter` | Finds the single vowel in selected words; general sorting and multiple-vowel words are not covered. |
| Consonant letter–sound correspondence | `r.lettersound` | Generated consonant correspondence. |
| Decode short-vowel words and sentences | `r.cvc`, `r1.sentence` | Word decoding and simple sentence comprehension; not every plan word set or sentence format. |
| Encode short-vowel words | `r.spell-short` | Selected short-vowel spelling choices; free spelling and the complete word lists remain. |
| Initial/final blends and digraphs: recognize, decode and spell | `r1.digraphs`, `r1.spell-blends`, `r1.spell-digraph` | Digraph decoding and selected blend and digraph spelling; initial/final sorting and the full word lists remain. |
| Silent e and vowel teams: decode, spell and read in sentences | `r1.silente`, `r1.vowelteams`, `r1.spell-vowel`, `r1.sentence` | Selected patterns and spelling choices; not all listed vowel spellings or sentence patterns. |
| R-controlled vowels: hearing, decoding and spelling | `r1.rcontrolled`, `r1.spell-r` | Hearing and selected spelling choices; independent reading of r-controlled sentences is missing. |
| Diphthongs and variant vowels | `r1.spell-variant` | Selected spelling choices; picture and sentence decoding and complete pattern coverage are missing. |
| Soft/hard c and g; silent letters; closed-syllable exceptions | `r1.soft-sounds` | Selected soft-c/g spellings only; hard/soft sorting, silent letters and exceptions remain. |
| Multisyllabic, open/closed and consonant-l-e words | `r1.syllable-join`, `r1.syllable-spell` | Selected word-part combinations and spellings including consonant-l-e; open/closed classification remains. |
| High-frequency words: read, match, spell and use in context | `r.sight`, `r1.sight-spell` | Selected recognition and spelling choices. The full sets, matching and contextual sentence completion are not covered. |
| Academic vocabulary: colors, numbers, location, nouns, verbs, adjectives and feelings | `r.vocab`, `r1.word-context` | Partial: categories, opposites and selected context clues; the full set of color, location, action and feeling tasks remains. |
| Word relationships: categories, synonyms, antonyms, shades of meaning | `r.vocab`, `r1.word-context` | Some categories, opposites and synonyms; ordered shades and all matching formats remain. |
| Homophones, multiple meanings and idioms | `r1.homophones`, `r1.word-context` | Selected homophones and contextual meanings; multiple-meaning picture tasks and idioms remain. |
| Roots, prefixes and suffixes | `r1.prefixes`, `r1.suffixes` | Selected meaning examples; identify base/prefix/suffix, other affixes and complete-the-word tasks remain. |
| Literature: characters, setting, events and picture connections | `r.lit` | Selected read-aloud stories and direct questions; picture matching, realistic versus fantasy and breadth of genre remain partial. |
| Literature: sequence and predictions | `r.story-order` | Selected sequence and prediction questions; no image-ordering interaction. |
| Literature: inference and character actions | `r.inference-k`, `r1.inference`, `r.lit` | Selected short scenarios and story questions; longer actions/dialogue tasks remain partial. |
| Literature: narrator and point of view | `r1.narrator` | Selected first-person examples; varied narrator forms remain. |
| Literature: lesson or theme | `r1.lesson` | Selected short scenarios; theme in longer myths, fables and legends remains. |
| Literature: read-aloud and independent stories across genres | `r.lit`, `r1.independent-stories`, `r2.readalone` | Independent Grade 1 short passages added; historical fiction, science fiction, myths and fables remain limited. |
| Informational text: main topic and details | `r.info`, `r1.independent-facts`, `r2.readalone` | Read-aloud and independent short texts; the complete range of subjects and questions is not present. |
| Informational text: text features and book parts | `r1.text-features` | Questions describe book features; navigation of real diagrams, contents pages and indexes is not yet interactive. |
| Informational text: author purpose | `r1.author-purpose` | Selected short descriptions, not full-length texts. |
| Informational text: cause/effect and comparison | `r1.cause-effect` | Selected brief examples; longer-text comparison remains. |
| Informational text: read-aloud/independent texts across science, biography, places, arts and other subjects | `r.info`, `r1.independent-facts`, `r2.readalone` | Selected science and nature facts; broader subjects and biographies remain. |

New skills use original prompts, separate authored practice and held-out mock questions, and do not change saved-state schema. “Show stretch skills” controls whether Grade 1 entries appear. Multiple-choice spelling assesses recognition of a spelling rather than unaided written spelling. Most new comprehension tasks are listened to; the two independent-reading skills present text without reading the passage or answer choices aloud. Four held-out items per new skill provide limited variety, so familiar-item evidence and repetition rules still apply.
