# India Readiness Plan

Goal: if the family moves to India, Aarav should be able to join a CBSE (or
Cambridge/IB) school at his age-appropriate class without a gap, especially in
**Hindi**, **math pace** and **India-specific general awareness**.

## 1. How the app handles it

The skill map is universal. Each curriculum is an *overlay* (`src/content/curricula.ts`):

| | USA · Georgia (current) | India · CBSE / NCERT |
|---|---|---|
| Grade names | K / Grade 1 / Grade 2 | UKG / Class 1 / Class 2 (approximate) |
| English | US spelling and accent | Indian/British spelling (colour, maths, biscuit, torch) and an Indian-English voice when available |
| Currency | $ | ₹ |
| Standard references | Georgia K-12 math codes, CCSS ELA | NCERT *Joyful Mathematics* Class 1 chapters (mapped by topic) |
| Region-only skills | — | Hindi Garden (5 skills), Rupees, India's symbols, India's festivals, Explore India |

**Settings → Curriculum** (parent dashboard): switch on both systems now, so he keeps i-Ready
prep for Forsyth *and* builds India readiness. "Main system" controls grade names, spelling and accent.

## 2. Age → class in India

Under NEP 2020 states are moving to **Class 1 at 6+ years (as on 31 March)**, with
Balvatika 1–3 (Nursery, LKG, UKG) for ages 3–6. Delhi is phasing this in from the 2026-27
session; CBSE schools generally follow the state rule. The Indian school year starts in **April**.

| Age on 31 March | Class in India (new rule) | Roughly the US equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 5+ | UKG / Balvatika-3 | Kindergarten |
| 6+ | Class 1 | Late K / Grade 1 |
| 7+ | Class 2 | Grade 1 / 2 |

Action: confirm the cut-off with the specific state and school once a city is known. Some
schools give a one-month relaxation; others do not.

## 3. Where the gaps will be (and what to do)

| Area | Expected gap | What the app does | What parents do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hindi** | Largest gap. CBSE schools teach Hindi from Class 1 (NCERT *Sarangi*); many expect vowel/consonant recognition and basic vocabulary | Hindi Garden: vowels अ–औ, consonants with picture words (क से कबूतर), everyday words, numbers 1–10, colours. All spoken by a Hindi voice | 10 min of Hindi conversation daily; grandparents on video calls; Hindi cartoons |
| **Math** | Small. NCERT Class 1 topics are covered by the K/G1 skills. Money is in ₹ | Rupees skill (coins, totals, change); NCERT chapter references in Skill map | Play shop with real rupee coins if available |
| **General awareness** | India symbols, festivals, geography are new | India's symbols, festivals, Explore India | Celebrate festivals with stories; India map puzzle |
| **English** | None in content; minor spelling/vocabulary differences | Indian/British spelling and accent when India is the main system | — |
| **Olympiads** | Very popular in India from Class 1 (e.g. SOF IMO/NSO/IEO) | Puzzle Cave + Brain teasers already train the reasoning | Consider one Olympiad in Class 1–2 for experience, not pressure |

## 5. Next India steps (roadmap)

1. **Hindi depth:** मात्रा (vowel signs: का, कि, की…), two- and three-letter words, tracing letters, then NCERT *Sarangi* Class 1 stories as listening comprehension.
2. **Equal groups** skill (Ch 11) and metric measurement (cm, kg, litre).
3. **EVS** (Environmental Studies) themes from NCERT Class 1–2: family, food, water, plants, animals, transport.
4. **Cambridge Primary overlay** for international schools in India, if one is chosen instead of CBSE.
5. **Human-recorded Hindi audio** to replace device voices, which vary in quality.
